# Constraint stage - Part of the Prompt Structure Framework
Specifiying constraints is highly useful as it let's AI know what to avoid which in turns helps it understanding what you want. 

## Guiding principle
Specifying constraints saves you time in editing, cleanup etc. Every constraint specified is a mistake that the AI will not make. If anything in the output is annoying you ex. EM dashes or other such characters etc. it's a good chance you missed specifying this as a constraint.

## Good example 
- Do not use jargon. Write at an 8th grade reading level.
- Do not suggest solutions that require a paid API. Everything should use free tools.
- Keep it under 300 words. No bullet points. Write in paragraphs.
- Do not start with "In today's world" or any variation of it.
